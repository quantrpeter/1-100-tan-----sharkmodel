T-34-76 model 1940 & model 1941

This covers all of the early 76mm versions with the "flat" cast and welded turrets.
(1942 & Hex turret versions to follow in seperate pack due to file-size issues. And yes, I'll probably revisit the T-34-85 soon as well.)

All factory-183 vehicles have the option of no fuel/ 4x box fuel tanks/ 10x box fuel tanks.
All STZ vehicles have no fuel tanks.
1940 vehicles have the option of 1940 welded or cast turrets.
1941 vehicles have the option of 1941 welded or cast turrets.
STZ vehicles can use either STZ or factory-183 1941 turrets.

* T-34-76 factory-183 1940 mid (L-11 gun, early hull front, early driver's hatch, 2 headlights, early intakes, early transmission hatch, early mudguards, plain hull, no boxes on mudguards, early idler/sprocket, early mg mount.)
* T-34-76 factory-183 1940 late (L-11 gun, early hull front, early driver's hatch, 2 headlights, early intakes, early transmission hatch, early mudguards, tiedowns on the hull, early idler/sprocket, early mg mount.)

* T-34-76 factory-183 1941 late (F-34 gun, early hull front, late driver's hatch, 1 headlight, late intakes, early transmission hatch, late mudguards, tiedowns on the  hull,boxes & track on mudguards, late idler/sprocket, early mg mount.)
* T-34-76 factory-183 1941 end (F-34 gun, cast steel wheels, late hull front, late driver's hatch, 1 headlight, late intakes, early transmission hatch, late mudguards, tiedowns on the hull,boxes & track on mudguards, late idler/sprocket, early mg mount.)
* T-34-76 factory-183 1941 late no mudguards (F-34 gun, early hull front, early driver's hatch, 1 headlight, early intakes, early transmission hatch, no mudguards, tiedowns on the hull, early idler/sprocket, early mg mount.)

* T-34-57 factory-183 1941 late 57mm no mudguards (ZIS-2 gun, early hull front, late driver's hatch, 2 headlights, late intakes, early transmission hatch, no mudguards, tiedowns on the hull, early idler/sprocket, early mg mount.)

* OT-34-76 factory-183 1941   1940 vehicle rebuilt as flamethrower (F-34 gun, early hull front, early driver's hatch, 2 headlights, early intakes, early transmission hatch, early mudguards, tiedowns on the hull, early idler/sprocket, flamethrower.) (Note that turret can be used on any 1940/1941 hull as for an updated machine.)

* T-34-76 factory-stz 1941 mid (F-34 gun, STZ turret, STZ rounded rear hull, cast steel wheels, late hull front, early driver's hatch, 1 headlight, late intakes, early transmission hatch, late mudguards, tiedowns on the hull,boxes on mudguards, late idler/sprocket, early mg mount.)
* T-34-76 factory-stz 1941 late (F-34 gun, STZ turret, STZ rounded rear hull, cast steel wheels, late hull front, late driver's hatch, 1 headlight, late intakes, early transmission hatch, late mudguards, plain hull,boxes on mudguards, late idler/sprocket, early mg mount.)
* T-34-76 factory-stz 1941 late no-mudguards (F-34 gun, STZ turret, STZ rounded rear hull, cast steel wheels, late hull front, late driver's hatch, 1 headlight, late intakes, early transmission hatch, no mudguards, plain hull, late idler/sprocket, early mg mount.)



All vehicles printed sitting on their tracks.
All turrets printed in correct orientation.


Remove raft then clear out tracks from the inside first. 
The tracks are deep, so you will need to cut gently underneath the body edge/mudguard to free the support - watch out for the mud flaps. Cut in towards the centre of the vehicle, not outwards.
