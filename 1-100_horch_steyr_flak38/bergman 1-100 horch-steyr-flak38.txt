Flak bases: FOW style (with recesses & plain), minimum sized rectangular base, no baseplate, no baseplate simple.

Vehicles printed sitting on their wheels, guns printed with gun horizontal.
Contrary to usual practice, clear the supports from the outsides of the wheels first. 
Take care around the front bumpers.
Cut down on each side of the Flak trunnion rings before pulling off the supports.
Cut down on each side of raised windows before removing supports.

Horch 108 portee designed as a click-in insert so that the model can be used as either a portee or normal personnel car.
Horch simple versions have solid windscreens.
Steyr simple versions have solid engine protection grille and solid windscreens.

Flak 38 simple is intended for modifying existing vehicles to flak configuration by gluing it into the vehicle's bed.
Flak models come with two different length spigots, short for the plain bases/trailer. 
(Short can actually be used for evertyhing if desired since the gun is well balanced.)
