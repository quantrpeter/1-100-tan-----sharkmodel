This model was a series of mistakes. I thought that since the Kurogane looked rather similar to the Austin, that it would be rather simple to morph one into the other. That turned out not to be the case...
And then I thought that the staff car would be a simple rear-end swap, which with smaller wheels, smaller doors, lower roof etc also turned out not to be the case.
But they are nice models.

All vehicles printed sitting on their wheels.
AA bren printed on its side.
