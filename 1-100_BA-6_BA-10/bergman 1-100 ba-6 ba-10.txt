Bergman 1-100 BA-3, BA-6, BA-10, BA-10M armoured cars

BA-3 & BA-6 are identical except for the door on the hull rear on the BA-3.
BA-10M differs from BA-10 in having fuel tanks on the rear mudguards, and the spare track 
shifted to the hull rear.

BA-3 - no track       (use BA-6 turret)
BA-3 - stowed track   (use BA-6 turret)
BA-3 - fitted track   (use BA-6 turret)

BA-6 - no track
BA-6 - stowed track
BA-6 - fitted track

BA-10 - no track
BA-10 - stowed track
BA-10 - fitted track

BA-10M - no track        (use BA-10 turret)
BA-10M - stowed track    (use BA-10 turret)
BA-10M - fitted track    (use BA-10 turret)

Vehicles printed sitting on their wheels, turrets printed with gun horizontal.
Contrary to usual practice, clear the supports from the outsides of the wheels first. 
Take care around the front bumper.
