A selection of Austin trucks with the common 1930s cab.                                                                                                              Very early vehicles had twin rear wheels, intended for road use only. Later vehicles had single rear off-road wheels.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
I have focused on GS versions because as far as I'm concerned I've covered the other assorted body styles more than adequately in other recent vehicles.
I intend to release a Coles crane pack at some later stage when I'm not overdosed any more on UK trucks.

K30, K3 early & K2Y have one-piece SLA versions available.


Austin K30 GS 4x2 30cwt truck   (open cab & soft cab roof.) (option of tilt raised.) Army. 

Austin K2 GS 4x2 2T truck       (closed cab.) (option of tilt raised.) RAF.

Austin K2Y 4x2 2T Ambulance     (option of open of closed cab.) Standard UK heavy ambulance. All services.

Austin K3 GS 4x2 3T truck       (Early, twin rear wheels) (open cab & soft cab roof.) (option of tilt raised.) Both Army & RAF.
Austin K3 GS 4x2 3T truck       (Early, twin rear wheels) (closed cab.) (option of tilt raised.)  RAF.

Austin K3 GS 4x2 3T truck       (Early) (open cab & soft cab roof.) (option of tilt raised.) Both Army & RAF.
Austin K3 GS 4x2 3T truck       (closed cab.) (option of tilt raised.)  RAF.


All vehicles printed sitting on their wheels.
Soft tops for K30 & K3 printed sideways so that straps are horizontal. Doing them this way resulted in problem-free printing.
Note that the K30 & K3 cab roofs are *NOT* interchangeable.
As always with wheeled vehicles, remove raft then clear out wheels from the outside first.
