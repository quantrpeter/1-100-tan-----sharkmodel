30' Scissors bridge carried on a modified Valentine II or III tank. A very successful combination.
Bridge weight capacity 30T. Time to launch bridge was 2 1/2 minutes.

Valentine Bridgelayer (choice of sand shields/no shields) (Choice of travel configuration or half-deployed.)
30' Scissors bridge. In two pieces, clips together and can fold.

There are assembled SLA versions which have the elevation gear in place. Bridge is still seperate.
For FDM, there are two versions of the elevation gear:
"Cutouts" has some infill of the ribs with big oval holes for better appearance. This is my recomendation.
"FDM" has solid sideways ribs for ease of printing.
