ANZAC Day 2018 set.
LRDG included many New Zealanders, and most of the trucks had Maori names painted on them.

Chevrolet (Canada) 1533x2 30cwt GS (Window up/down, bumper bar/no bar)
Chevrolet (Canada) 1533x2 30cwt LRDG (Centre pedestal mount/rear pedestal mount, bumper bar/no bar) Large choice of weapons to glue in.
Chevrolet (Canada) 1533x2 30cwt LRDG with Breda 20mm (bumper bar/no bar))

Chevrolet 1543x2 3t GS (open/closed/fully closed, spare/no spare, bumper bar/no bar)

Simple versions of all models included (no recess behind wheels).
Weapons on LRDG 30cwt are mix-and-match, but centre mount was most common for heavy weapons (30cal, 50cal, Boy) while rear mount mount was mainly Lewis or K-gun. 
With rear mount, Boy sometimes seen mounted on front wall of cargo box, behind cab.

All vehicles printed sitting on their wheels.
All small weapons printed on their side.
Breda printed with gun horizontal, in actual orientation.
As always with wheeled vehicles, remove raft then clear out wheels from the outside first. Take care around the mud flaps and front bumper bar.
Clearing out open cab can be tricky (cut down beside steering column both sides) so try to minimise supports on open models.
