Holt 75  (75HP tractor)
Holt 120  (120HP tractor)

The ancestor of most WWI tanks, but also widely used by all the allied forces as a heavy artillery tractor.
Canopy is optional. There is an alternate version with the side panels unfolded.
Simple versions included of tractors and frames.
Frames are intended to have the arms folded after printing. The ones labeled folded have the arms in the folded positions, mainly for SLA use.

All vehicles printed sitting on their tracks.
All frames printed flat.
All canopys printed flat.
Remove raft then clear out tracks from the inside first.
