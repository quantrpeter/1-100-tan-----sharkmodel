Bergman 1-100 Trippel SG-6/38 & SG-6/41 Amphibian


Please remember: CC BY-SA-NC license.

Trippel amphibian, much more sophisticated than the Schwimmwagen, but also much more expensive. Mainly produced for the SS.


Trippel SG-6/38  initial 1938 version.   Open & Closed versions. (Spare wheel optional.)
Trippel SG-6/41  improved 1941 version.  Open & Closed versions. (Spare wheel optional.)

All vehicles printed sitting on their wheels.
Remove raft then clear out between wheels first.
SG-6/38 FDM version fills in under steering wheel, since that can be difficult to remove the supports from.
SG-6/38 Simple version fills in under the rail on the rear deck.
Do the windows last for open versions. Cut down alongside windows with a sharp blade, inside and outside.
