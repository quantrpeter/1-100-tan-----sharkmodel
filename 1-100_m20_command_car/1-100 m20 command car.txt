M20 Command Car (Early)   Mine racks on side.
M20 Command Car (Mid)     Petrol can racks on side.
M20 Command Car (Late)    Storage bins on side, windscreen storage box on glacis.


All versions have the option of full guards, front guards only, rear guards only, or no guards.

MG ring glues onto hull top. The location should be obvious.
The HMG has optional straight-ahead and turned-to-the-side options.

All vehicles printed sitting on their wheels.
HMG printed on its side. MG ring printed in its correct orientation, but upside-down works well too.
Remove raft then clear out wheels from the inside first. You may need to cut gently underneath the guards on the removed versions to free the support. Minimise the support angle to prevent the interior being filled up.
