Simple versions of the two open models are included, with solid windscreens.

Vehicles printed sitting on their wheels.
As always with wheeled vehicles, it is easier to remove the supports from the outside of the wheels first.
Take care around the bumpers.
The open mk3 with hollowed out front window is tricky, and I suggest you use the simple model unless you are confident.
