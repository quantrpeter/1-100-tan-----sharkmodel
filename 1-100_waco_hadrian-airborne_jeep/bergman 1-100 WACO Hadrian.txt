WACO Hadrian CG-4 (in flight)  Use:
hadrian landing gear LH 2
hadrian landing gear RH 2
hadrian wing brace LH 2
hadrian wing brace RH 2
hadrian wing LH 6
hadrian wing RH 6
hadrian 14 body (one piece entire fuselage) *OR* hadrian 14 bodytemp + hadrian 14 tailtemp + hadrian 14 nosetemp closed

WACO Hadrian CG-4 (landed)  Use:
hadrian 14 bodytemp
hadrian 14 tailtemp
hadrian 14 nosetemp open *OR* hadrian 14 nosetemp closed
hadrian landing gear collapsed LH 2
hadrian landing gear collapsed RH 2
hadrian wing brace LH 2
hadrian wing brace RH 2
hadrian wing LH 6 *OR* hadrian wing LH broken 6 & hadrian wing LH broken tip 6
hadrian wing RH 6 *OR* hadrian wing RH broken 6 & hadrian wing RH broken tip 6
Glue a piece of (preferably transparent) dia. 1.75 filament onto the bottom of hadrian 14 bodytemp, near the tail joint,
and trim the length until the glider sits on its skids & wheels

Use whatever orientation seems best to you, except:
Suggest you print the wings flat (rotate them 2 degrees from default position to get them flat.)
Print wing braces flat for maximum strength.

The testers used a variety of orientations, in 1:100 and 1:56, and they all came out fine.

Vehicles printed sitting on their wheels.
Cut down on each side of the MG before pulling off the supports.
