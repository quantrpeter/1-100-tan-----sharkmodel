Bergman 1-100 Marmon Herrington Mk1 & Mk2 Armoured Cars

Mk3 & Mk4 will be covered in a later pack.

Please remember: CC BY-SA-NC license.

MFF version was South African-specific version with Vickers MG in turret.
The standard version (ME version) had Boys AT Rifle + Bren MG in turret.

South African Armoured Car Mk1 is technically not a "Marmon Herrington" since they were based on a 2WD Ford chassis and did not use the Marmon Herrington 4WD conversion set, but I've retained the customary names.

Marmon Herrington Armoured Car Mk2 (welded) HQ / Engineer version with turret removed

All models printed sitting on their wheels. Turrets and guns printed with gun barrels horizontal.
The guns are the trickiest bits to clean up - take it slowly and carefully and you should be fine. There is a simple version of the Breda 20mm with the holes filled in.
All three turret versions have an open-hatch variant.
