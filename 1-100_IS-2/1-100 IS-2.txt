IS-1  (with/without 4 jettison tanks)  85mm gun

IS-2 "broken nose" early  (with/without 4 jettison tanks, 6 tracklinks)  122mm gun with narrow mantlet
IS-2 "broken nose"        (with/without 4 jettison tanks, 6 tracklinks)  122mm gun with wide mantlet

IS-2 late  (with/without 4 jettison tanks, log, 4 tracklinks, short/full guards, AA MG)  122mm gun with wide, notched mantlet, scalloped cupola

IS-2M  (with/without log, AA MG)  reshaped hull, full guards


I'm using IS-2M (capital M) to refer to the postwar rebuilds, and I'm using IS-2 late to refer to IS-2m (small M), to avoid confusion.

All vehicles printed sitting on their tracks.
All turrets printed with gun barrels horizontal.
Clean out between tracks underneath first (cut down beside inside of tracks), or else you'll have real trouble clearing around the wheels.
