SAS Jeep (Europe, armoured)

SAS desert jeeps were pretty uniform in can stowage, but varied wildly in armament. Pretty much any combination can go. (Excepting 2x 0.5" HMG.)

SAS Europe jeeps appear to be armed almostly exclusively with Vickers K guns, the only real variation being a choice of single or double guns in the rear.
SAS armoured Europe jeeps appear to be armed with Vickers K guns (armoured in front), with unarmoured single or double guns in the rear.

The simple versions don't have the front mudguard braces, and solid armour windows on the armour variant.

Vehicles printed sitting on their wheels, Most guns printed with gun horizontal, on their sides. 
The exception is the double front guns, which I printed with the gun barrels horizontal, and the gun upright.
Contrary to usual practice, clear the supports from the outsides of the wheels first. 
The double guns on arm are the most tricky to clean up. Clip as close as posible to the guns all round and be gentle.
