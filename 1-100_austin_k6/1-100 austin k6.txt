Common 6-wheel Austin truck with military cab.                                                                                                              Very Early vehicles had twin rear wheels, intended for road use only. Later vehicles had single rear off-road wheels.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
I have focused on GS versions because as far as I'm concerned I've covered the other assorted body styles more than adequately in other recent vehicles.
I intend to release a Coles crane pack at some later stage when I'm not overdosed any more on UK trucks.

Austin K6 6x4 Breakdown Gantry  (hatch in cab roof.) (option of rear flaps open.) (option of travelling, towing and crane positions for gantry.) All services.

All vehicles printed sitting on their wheels.
Minimise supports on the open K6 breakdown gantry models, and the interior comes out without too much effort.
As always with wheeled vehicles, remove raft then clear out wheels from the outside first.
