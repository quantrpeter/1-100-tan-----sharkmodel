Bergman 1-100 M3A1 Scout Car

Please remember: CC BY-SA-NC license.

American Scout car, used by almost all Allied nations. Russian vehicles sometimes had roller removed.


Models included:

M3A1 early    (No fuel cans, spotlights) with/without aerial hole, open/closed.
M3A1          with/without aerial hole, open/closed, with/without roller.
M3A1 late     ( blackout light) with/without aerial hole, open/closed.

I've included a raised-screws version of everything, although I prefer the smooth look myself.

Aerial mast glues into aerial hole. (aerial points forwards.)
.50 cal hmg and .30 cal Mg (air-cooled for mid/late, watercooled for early) glue onto skate rail as desired.
Most common weapons fit 1x HMG and 1-2x MG


All vehicles printed sitting on their wheels.
Guns printed with gun horizontal, but might be easier printed on their side.
Remove raft then clear out wheels from outside first.
Aerial hole may need opening up.
