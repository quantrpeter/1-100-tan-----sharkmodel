Alecto

Tetrarch hull comes in simple and complex versions.
I recommend using the simple ones for 1:100 unless you realy like a challenge.
However for 1:56 the complex one should be easier to clean, and that complicated suspension looks better.

Vehicles printed sitting on their wheels.
Turrets printed with gun barrels horizontal.
It is vital to clear out between the wheels underneath the hull before removing the outside supports.
Suggest you cut down beside each track on the inside, which makes it much easier.
Cut down on each side of the Alecto gun shield inside the fighting compartment to release the inside supports easily.
