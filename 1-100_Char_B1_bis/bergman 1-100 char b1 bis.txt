Bergman 1-100 Char B1 / Char B1 bis family

Please remember: CC BY-SA-NC license.

Char B1              (different turret, short 47mm gun, tow hook)
Char B1 bis          (revised engine deck, different turret with longer 47mm gun)
Pzkpfw B-2 740(f)    Char B1 bis in German service. (Different tool layout, jacks, aerial mounts) also with optional German-modified cupola on turret.
Flammwagen auf Pzkpfw B-2(f) early     Initial version flamethrower vehicle. Served on Eastern front only. (Small flame turret as per Pzkpfw II Flamm)
Flammwagen auf Pzkpfw B-2(f)           Second version flamethrower vehicle. Served on Western front only. (Big visor, large fuel tank on rear hull)
10.5cm leFH18/3 auf GW B-2(f)          105mm howitzer conversion of Char B1 bis. (Howitzer is a seperate part)

All vehicles except the Char B1 have an option of chains hanging on the rear plate.
All of the above vehicles have a simple version, which fills in under the trackguards for ease of cleanup.


All models printed sitting on their tracks. Main guns printed with gun barrels horizontal, in the same orientation as the hulls.
