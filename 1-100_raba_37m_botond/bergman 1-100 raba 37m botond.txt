Raba 37M Botond I & Botond II  - highly regarded Hungarian artillery tractor. Botond II had LHD, minor changes around bonnet and a larger engine.

Versions included:
Botond I (cab & body open)  (With/without hoops for roof).  Window is seperate piece, can be glued up or down.
Botond I (cab open).  Window is seperate piece, can be glued up or down.
Botond I (cab closed).
Botond I (Cab and body closed).

Botond II (cab & body open)  (With/without hoops for roof).  Window is seperate piece, can be glued up or down.
Botond II (cab open).  Window is seperate piece, can be glued up or down.
Botond II (cab closed).
Botond II (Cab and body closed).

Simple version of everything included, which has the underside flattened off as much as possible without being visible.

All models printed sitting on their wheels. Simple versions have reduced underside detail to make for easier cleanup.
Take care around the front bumpers. As always with these wheeled vehicles, clear out the wheels from the outside first.
For open cab vehicles, clear off the top of the steering wheel, then cut down beside it to break the internal raft. Then the blob under the steering wheel should be easy to remove.
